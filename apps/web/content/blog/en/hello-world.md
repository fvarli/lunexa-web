This is the first entry in Lunexa's notebook.

We spend most of our time inside client projects, where the interesting decisions never leave the engineering channel. That's the trade-off of consulting work: good decisions happen, but they don't compound in public. This space exists to change that — a little.

## What you'll find here

Short posts, written when we have something specific to say:

- **Craft notes** — the small decisions that separate a product that feels right from one that merely works. Type choices, loading states, error recovery patterns, cache invalidation strategies.
- **Tooling reports** — when we adopt or abandon a tool, why. No affiliate links, no hot takes, just what actually made a difference.
- **Incident post-mortems** — published when we break something interesting and the lesson generalizes.
- **Pattern libraries** — reusable solutions to problems we've seen more than twice.

## What you won't find

Listicles. Thought-leadership essays with no specific claim. Content recycled from other sites. AI-generated filler — every post here is written by a human at Lunexa, edited by another human, and published because it helps the reader do something they couldn't before.

## Cadence

One post per month at minimum. More when we have something to say.

Subscribe to the [newsletter](/#newsletter) to get new posts in your inbox. We won't spam, and the unsubscribe link is in every email.

Thanks for reading.
